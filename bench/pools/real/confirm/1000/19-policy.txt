Please go through this policy for me in plain language, section by section, and then tell me what the weak spots and risks are. I run a small workshop that makes wooden and fabric toys and our retailer asked us to adopt a product safety and returns policy before the winter season. I would like to know what it commits us to and whether anything is unrealistic for a team of nine.

TOY SAFETY AND RETURNS POLICY
Tumbleweed Toy Company Ltd. Version 2.0, effective 1 November 2026.

1. Scope
This policy applies to every toy designed, made or sold under the Tumbleweed name, including products sold through retailers and at markets. It applies to all employees, temporary staff and contract makers.

2. Materials
2.1 All wood must come from suppliers who can supply a certificate of origin. All paints, dyes and varnishes must be water based and certified as safe for toys intended for children under three.
2.2 Fabric must be tested for colourfastness and flammability by an accredited laboratory before the first production run of any new design.
2.3 Small parts that fit inside a cylinder of 31.7 mm by 57.1 mm are not allowed in any toy marked for children under three.

3. Testing and records
3.1 The production lead must carry out a pull test and a drop test on one item from every batch of 50 and write the result in the batch log.
3.2 Batch logs, supplier certificates and laboratory reports must be kept for ten years.
3.3 Any change of supplier or material requires a new laboratory report before the changed product is sold.

4. Complaints and incidents
4.1 Any report of injury, choking or an allergic reaction must be passed to the managing director within one working day.
4.2 The company must stop selling the affected product within two working days while the cause is investigated.
4.3 Where an injury is serious, the company shall tell the trading standards authority within three days.

5. Returns
5.1 Customers may return any toy within 60 days for a full refund, whether or not it has been used, provided they have proof of purchase.
5.2 Toys returned because of a safety concern shall be quarantined and shall not be resold.
5.3 The company shall refund the cost of postage on all safety returns.

6. Recalls
6.1 If a defect is found that could cause serious harm, the company shall recall all affected units, notify retailers within 24 hours and publish a notice on its website.
6.2 The cost of the recall shall be paid by the company.

7. Training
7.1 Every new employee and contract maker must complete a one-hour safety induction in their first week, covering small parts, materials and the batch log.
7.2 The production lead must repeat the induction for all staff each October, before the winter season.

8. Suppliers
8.1 The company shall ask each supplier to sign a statement that their materials meet the standards in clause 2.
8.2 The company shall visit or audit each main supplier at least once every two years.
8.3 A supplier that fails an audit shall be removed from the approved list until the faults are corrected and confirmed in writing.

9. Labelling
9.1 Every toy shall carry the age range, the batch number and the company's contact details on the toy or its packaging.
9.2 Toys with small parts that are not suitable for children under three shall carry a clear warning in words and a symbol.

10. Review
This policy shall be reviewed every year by the managing director and the production lead.
