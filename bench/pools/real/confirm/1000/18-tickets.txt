Three tickets landed in the Saltmarsh Light Trust inbox today and I need a full, friendly reply to each one that I can send without editing much. Say what we will do, what they need to do, and when. If something is not in my notes below, flag it at the end of that reply rather than inventing it.

My notes: visitor tours of Skerry Head Light run April to October, Tuesday to Sunday, at 10:30, 13:00 and 15:00, adults 9, children 5, under fives free. Tours are limited to 14 people because of the spiral stair (118 steps). The tower is closed in strong winds, and in that case we refund or rebook at no cost. Dogs are welcome in the grounds but only assistance dogs go up the tower. The keeper's cottage tearoom is run by a separate tenant, not by us.

Extra information from the volunteer coordinator: after the wind closures on 3 October we contacted everyone with a booking by email except those who booked by phone, and a refund of the 46 pounds can be issued within five working days. The Trust is considering a ground-floor exhibition in the old oil store, with a film and a model of the lamp, which is open to wheelchair users, but it has not been scheduled yet. The lost property box is kept in the shop and checked every Monday. Volunteer guides complete a two-day induction in March and a first-aid course, and applications open in January.
