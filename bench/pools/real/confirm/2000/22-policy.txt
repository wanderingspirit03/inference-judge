SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This agreement is made on 2 October 2026 between Wenslow Fold Dairy Ltd, whose registered office is at Fold Farm, Wenslow Bank, Eastmere (the "Supplier"), and Candlewick Provisions Ltd, whose registered office is at Unit 9, Harbour Yard, Northquay (the "Buyer").

1. Products and volumes
1.1 The Supplier shall supply to the Buyer the following products: Wenslow Clothbound Cheddar in 20 kg wheels, Fold Blue in 2.5 kg rounds, and Tarn Fresh Curd in 1 kg tubs (together the "Products").
1.2 The Buyer shall order a minimum of 4 tonnes of Products in each calendar quarter (the "Minimum Quantity"). The Supplier shall not be obliged to supply more than 7 tonnes in any quarter.
1.3 If the Buyer orders less than the Minimum Quantity in a quarter, the Buyer shall pay the Supplier a shortfall charge equal to 60 percent of the price of the unordered quantity.

2. Orders and delivery
2.1 The Buyer shall place each order in writing at least 14 days before the delivery date it requires.
2.2 The Supplier shall deliver to the Buyer's warehouse at Harbour Yard on the agreed date, between 6 a.m. and 2 p.m. Delivery is at the Supplier's cost and risk until the Products are unloaded.
2.3 If the Supplier delivers more than 2 working days late, the Buyer may reduce the price of the late consignment by 5 percent for each further day of delay, up to 30 percent.

3. Price
3.1 The prices are set out in Schedule 1 and are fixed until 30 September 2027.
3.2 After that date the Supplier may increase prices once in each year of this agreement by giving 60 days' written notice, provided the increase does not exceed the percentage change in the published food producer price index over the previous twelve months.
3.3 The Buyer shall pay each invoice within 60 days of the end of the month in which the invoice is dated.
3.4 Interest on late payments shall accrue at 2 percent above the base rate of the Bank of England.

4. Quality and rejection
4.1 The Supplier warrants that the Products are made from pasteurised or raw milk produced on the Supplier's own farm, comply with all food safety law, and are within their stated shelf life on delivery, with at least 70 percent of the shelf life remaining.
4.2 The Buyer may reject any consignment that does not meet clause 4.1 by giving notice within 3 working days of delivery. The Supplier shall at its own cost collect the rejected Products and, at the Buyer's choice, replace them or refund the price.
4.3 The Buyer may inspect the dairy and the maturing rooms on 2 working days' notice, and the Supplier shall keep batch and temperature records for 3 years.

5. Exclusivity
5.1 During the term the Supplier shall not sell Wenslow Clothbound Cheddar to any other retailer or wholesaler within 40 miles of Northquay.
5.2 The Buyer may buy similar products from other suppliers.

6. Intellectual property and branding
6.1 The Supplier keeps all rights in the names Wenslow, Fold Blue and Tarn Fresh Curd and in its recipes and labels.
6.2 The Buyer may use the Supplier's names and photographs supplied by the Supplier in its shops, catalogue and website for the term and for 6 months afterwards. The Buyer may not alter the labels or repackage the Products without the Supplier's written consent.

7. Liability
7.1 The Supplier shall be liable for any loss, cost or claim suffered by the Buyer arising from the Products, including loss of profit and the cost of any product recall, with no upper limit.
7.2 The Buyer's liability to the Supplier under this agreement is limited to the price of the Products ordered in the previous 12 months.
7.3 The Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of at least 2 million pounds.

8. Force majeure
8.1 Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control. This does not include disease in the Supplier's herd, shortage of milk, or the failure of the Supplier's equipment.
8.2 If an event under this clause lasts longer than 30 days, either party may end this agreement.

9. Term and termination
9.1 This agreement starts on 1 November 2026 and continues for 3 years unless ended earlier under this clause.
9.2 Either party may end it for serious breach that is not remedied within 21 days of written notice.
9.3 The Buyer may end this agreement at any time by giving 90 days' written notice. The Supplier may end it only at the end of the initial 3 year term, by giving 6 months' written notice.
9.4 On expiry or termination, the Buyer may sell its remaining stock of the Products.

10. Recall and traceability
10.1 If either party believes that any of the Products may be unsafe, it shall tell the other by telephone and email within 4 hours of becoming aware, and the parties shall cooperate in any recall.
10.2 The Buyer shall record the batch code of every wheel, round and tub it sells on and shall give the Supplier those records within 24 hours of a request.
10.3 The Supplier shall pay the reasonable cost of any recall of the Products, including the cost of collecting stock from the Buyer's customers and of notices in the press, whether or not the Supplier was at fault.
10.4 If the Buyer fails to keep the records in clause 10.2, it shall not be entitled to any refund under clause 4.2 for the batch concerned.

11. Assignment and subcontracting
11.1 The Supplier may not assign this agreement or subcontract any part of the making, maturing or transport of the Products without the Buyer's written consent.
11.2 The Buyer may assign this agreement to any company in its group, or to a buyer of its business, on written notice to the Supplier. The Supplier's obligations continue in favour of the new owner.

12. Confidentiality
12.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this agreement and the other's business information confidential for the term and for 5 years afterwards.

13. General
13.1 Notices shall be sent by post to the registered office or by email to the contact listed in Schedule 2. A notice sent by email is treated as received on the next working day.
13.2 This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales and any dispute shall be decided by the courts of England and Wales.
13.3 This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both.
