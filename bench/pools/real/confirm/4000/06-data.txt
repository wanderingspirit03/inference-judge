Notes from the secretary. Licence fees are 22 pounds per adult and 14 pounds per junior, which gives about 25,400 pounds for 2026 and matches the accounts, although I have not checked it line by line. The Table 2 totals are what I got when I added the months up myself, so please check them. Hollis Bridge ran only one event but has the highest rating and the most women members. Three clubs (Brannock Rooks, Dunmere Knights, Nethering) hosted no events at all, while Corvale hosted five after joining us only three years ago. Two big clubs, Brannock Rooks and Dunmere Knights, each lost 11 members, and Thornby lost 12. The rating bands in Table 7 are rough because ratings of players with fewer than ten rated games are provisional. February and May look weak in Table 2, and I do not know whether that is exam season, bad weather, or because some entries were never recorded. The youth programme grant comes from the Northmere Community Trust and ends in March 2027, and the sponsor who paid 9,000 pounds in 2024-25 has told me he will probably give less again next year. The board wants to decide in November whether to cut the prize fund by a quarter, spend more on junior coaching, or both.
