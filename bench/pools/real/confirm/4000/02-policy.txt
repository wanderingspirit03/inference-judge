4.3 You may ask for one free extension of 48 hours per parcel through the app, provided you ask before the Collection Window ends. We may refuse an extension at any time if the unit is nearly full.

4.4 If a parcel has not been collected after 14 days from the time of delivery we treat it as abandoned and clause 10 applies.
