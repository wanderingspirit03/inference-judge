OUR STANDARD TERMS (for reference):
- Every car is sold with a 90-day / 3,000-mile warranty covering engine, gearbox, clutch, steering, brakes and electrical system. Tyres, wipers, bulbs, brake pads and discs, batteries and cosmetic damage are not covered.
- Under the warranty, we repair at our workshop or at an approved garage; repairs elsewhere must be approved by us first, in writing.
- Customers who buy at a distance (online or phone) have 14 days after delivery to cancel and return the car, provided it has been driven no more than 200 miles and is returned in the same condition. Customers who buy in person at the showroom do not have this right, but we offer a 7-day exchange on the same terms if they have a problem that is not their fault.
- Part-exchange values are agreed in writing at the time of sale and are fixed.
- Finance is arranged through an independent broker, Calderon Finance; we do not make decisions on credit or settle finance agreements ourselves.
- Our complaints process: we acknowledge within 2 working days and give a full answer within 15 working days.
- Showroom hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. Workshop: Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:30. Phone: 01632 960 114.
