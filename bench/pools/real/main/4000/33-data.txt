## Background

Harbour Row Block C is a six-storey residential block with 54 apartments and one level of basement parking, built for Calder & Rowe Developments. Our site office is the main contractor. Start on site was Monday 2 March 2026 and the contract completion date is 30 October 2026, which is 35 weeks. The tables below cover weeks 1 to 18, from 2 March to 5 July 2026.

Planned cumulative percentage comes from the baseline programme approved in February. Actual cumulative percentage is the quantity surveyor's monthly valuation interpolated by week, so treat it as rounded. Average workers per day is taken from the signing-in sheets, and it includes subcontractor staff but not deliveries drivers or visitors. Weather hours lost is the total of lost working hours recorded in the site diary, counting only hours when the foreman stopped a named activity. Concrete is the volume placed that week in cubic metres (basement slab, columns, and the floor slabs up to level 3, plus the level 4 slab in week 14). Late deliveries are deliveries that arrived more than two hours after the booked slot or on a different day. Reportable incidents are those that had to be reported to the safety authority; near misses are the ones logged on the site card system. Overtime hours are those paid at premium rate across all directly employed labour.

## Notes from the site manager

- The programme assumed a 5-day working week with no weekend work. Since week 10, Saturday working has been used on and off, which explains most of the overtime figures.
- Week 6 had the storm that stopped the crane for three days. The concrete pour planned for that week moved to week 7.
- The tower crane was off site for inspection for two days in week 12, and I have not entered those hours in the weather column because it was not weather.
- The level 4 slab thickness (D-019) is being checked by the structural engineer. If it needs to be corrected by adding a screed topping, the programme impact is estimated at about 5 working days, but this is not confirmed.
- The client has asked for a revised completion forecast at the board meeting. Our own estimate in week 18 is that completion will move to late December unless we recover time, but I do not trust it.
