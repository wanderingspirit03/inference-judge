--- Internal note added by standards officer (Tuesday 10:15) ---
v4 is compulsory for all egg suppliers from 1 January; the board decided in June. Stocking density is measured on usable area, excluding nest boxes and feeders. Kettleby sheds 400 and 410 usable gross, but the audit counts usable area only, which is typically about 92 per cent of gross. Verandah (covered veranda area) is required to be at least 10 per cent of the house floor area, which for 810 square metres gross is 81 square metres. Transitional support: members with fewer than 5,000 birds can apply to the Rural Improvement Fund for up to 40 per cent of costs, applications close 30 November. A first failed audit gives a 90 day corrective period; the contract is only suspended if the second audit also fails.

=== POLICY NOTES FOR REPLIES (support team use) ===

1. Missed milk collections: if the cooperative fails to collect, milk that has to be discarded is compensated at 85 per cent of the average milk price for the month, on production of the tank record or a photograph of the dump. The average milk price for the month in question is 38.5 pence per litre. Support can approve compensation up to 500 pounds; above that, the finance manager must approve.
2. Payment queries: statements are corrected within 5 working days once an error is confirmed, and the correction appears on the next weekly payment run (Fridays). Support may not reverse a payment adjustment without written confirmation from the relevant packhouse.
3. Onboarding: the member number is assigned only once the joining share is allocated. Support can correct the email address on a record after phone verification of the member's postcode and farm name. Members who cannot use the portal can place orders by phone on 01632 960 118, 08:00 to 17:00 on weekdays, and the early seed discount can be honoured by phone order up to the deadline.
4. Standards: members should be sent the v4 standards guide and offered a free pre-audit visit. Support must not give opinions about whether a farm will pass.
5. Wholesale customers: short deliveries are credited at the invoiced price on the next invoice. Missing invoices are reissued the same day to the address the customer specifies.
6. Complaints: formal complaints are acknowledged in writing within 2 working days and answered within 15 working days by the operations manager. Support should acknowledge, explain what happens next, and not promise any outcome.
7. Shared machinery: if the cooperative cancels a booking because of breakdown, the member is offered the earliest free slot and, on receipt of invoices from helpers or contractors that were already committed, up to 150 pounds towards documented costs. Anything higher needs the machinery coordinator's approval. Members are not charged hire for cancelled slots.
