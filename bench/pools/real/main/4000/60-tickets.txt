OUR POLICIES (Sorrel & Fern Tea Traders)
- Orders ship from our warehouse in 2 working days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within the country and 7 to 12 abroad. Shipping is free over 45 within the country and over 90 abroad.
- Returns: unopened tins and pouches can be returned within 30 days for a full refund of the goods (the customer pays the return postage). Opened items cannot be returned unless faulty or wrong.
- Damaged or wrong items: we replace or refund in full, including postage, with no return needed if the item is worth under 25. A photo is required.
- Subscriptions: billed on the 1st of each month, can be paused or cancelled any time before the 28th of the previous month. Cancelling after that date means the next box is still sent.
- Gift vouchers are valid for 24 months and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Our tea is stored in a cool dry warehouse; best-before dates are printed on the base of each tin or the back of each pouch. Customs duties abroad are the customer's responsibility.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 working days.
