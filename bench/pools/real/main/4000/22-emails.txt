Thanks Odile. My view as technical volunteer, short version:

The tower owner's price is too high for what we get. The Hollin Hill site covers about 60 percent of the town and the valley, but the valley side is the part with fewer listeners. I have measured signal on the walking route between Pennyfield and Marsh End and the signal there is poor anyway. I think we should ask the tower owner for a two year deal at 3,300 a year. If they refuse, we can move the antenna to the roof of the Fenwick Library, which has offered us a free spot. The library roof is lower and we would lose maybe 25 percent of coverage in the north, but the south and the town centre, where most of our listeners live, would improve slightly.

On the grant, I need the money to arrive before we can order anything, and the equipment supplier says lead time is five weeks. So if the decision is 31 October and we have to spend by 31 March, there is no problem, but we should not start spending our own matching money until we know.

On the schedule, I would rather we run automated music in the gaps than have bad shows. Our best hours are the evening slots where we have a real person and a theme.

Teodor
