PRODUCT SPEC: Berth Booking and Landing Declarations (v1.2), Port of Selmouth Harbour Authority

1. Overview
Fishing skippers use a web page and a mobile app to reserve a berth, declare landings and pay harbour dues. Harbour staff use a back-office screen to approve and audit declarations.

2. Berths
2.1 The harbour has 46 berths in three zones: A (large trawlers, 14 berths, up to 24 m), B (medium boats, 20 berths, up to 15 m) and C (small boats, 12 berths, up to 9 m).
2.2 A vessel may use a berth in its own zone or in a larger zone, never a smaller one.
2.3 Reservations run for a minimum of 12 hours and a maximum of 14 days. Reservations may not overlap on the same berth.
2.4 Visiting vessels (registered in another harbour) may stay at most 7 nights in any 30 days.

3. Accounts
3.1 A skipper registers with a vessel registration number, a vessel length and an email address. The registration number must match the national register through a lookup service. If the lookup service is down, the registration is held as "pending" and the skipper can look but not book.
3.2 One account can hold up to 3 vessels.

4. Landing declarations
4.1 A declaration must be submitted within 2 hours of landing and always before the catch leaves the harbour area.
4.2 It lists species, weight in kilograms (to one decimal place) and the fishing area code. Species come from a fixed list of 60 codes.
4.3 The weight total per declaration must not exceed the vessel's quota for the species. Quotas are loaded each Monday from a spreadsheet by an administrator. If a declaration would exceed a quota, it is accepted but flagged for inspection, and the skipper sees a warning.
4.4 Declarations cannot be edited after submission. A correction is made by a new declaration marked "amendment" that links to the original and needs staff approval.

5. Dues and payment
5.1 Berth dues are 1.20 per metre of vessel length per night, plus 8 percent of the first sale value for catches of shellfish landed on site. Dues are calculated at the end of the stay.
5.2 Payment is by card within 14 days. Overdue accounts cannot make new reservations.
5.3 A vessel that departs earlier than reserved is charged for the reserved nights unless it cancels at least 24 hours before.

6. Notifications
Skippers receive an email when a reservation is confirmed, 24 hours before arrival, and when a declaration is flagged. Weather warnings for gale force 8 or above are sent to all vessels with a current reservation.

7. Roles
Skipper, Harbour clerk (can approve amendments and see all declarations), Inspector (read-only, can export declarations as CSV) and Administrator (quota upload, berth management).

8. Non-functional
Pages load in under 3 seconds on a mobile connection. The mobile app must work offline for declarations and send them when a signal returns. All times are shown in local time with daylight saving handled correctly.
