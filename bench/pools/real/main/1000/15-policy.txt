I got this letter and policy from our water company about a new payment arrangement. Could you explain it clause by clause in plain language, and then tell me which clauses are risks for me as a customer and what I should ask them to change or clarify before I sign?

**Marlow Valley Water - Instalment and Leak Allowance Policy (version 3)**

**1. Who this applies to.** This policy applies to household customers with a metered supply. It does not apply to businesses, landlords with more than three properties, or customers whose account is in arrears for more than 90 days.

**2. Instalment plan.** A customer whose bill is more than 150 pounds may ask to pay it in up to twelve equal monthly instalments by direct debit. The company will add a plan fee of 1.5 percent of the outstanding balance, charged once at the start. The first instalment is taken within 14 days of the plan being agreed.

**3. Missed payments.** If a direct debit fails, the company will try again once, five days later. If the second attempt fails, the plan ends, the remaining balance becomes due in full within 14 days, and a late fee of 25 pounds is added. The company may then pass the account to a collection agency.

**4. Leak allowance.** If a customer finds and repairs a hidden leak on the supply pipe between the meter and the house, the company will, on request, charge for the extra water used at the normal sewerage-free rate, instead of the full rate, for up to 90 days before the repair. The customer must send a plumber's invoice within 60 days of the repair. The allowance can be granted only once in any five year period and does not cover leaks from taps, toilets, appliances or garden hoses.

**5. Estimated bills.** Where the company cannot read the meter, it may issue an estimated bill based on the average of the previous twelve months. Customers may submit their own reading at any time. When a true reading is later taken, any difference is added to or taken from the next bill.

**6. Changes to the policy.** The company may change this policy at any time by publishing the new version on its website. The new version applies to existing plans from 30 days after publication unless the customer cancels the plan in writing within that period.

**7. Disputes.** A customer who disagrees with a bill must tell the company within 12 months of the bill date. The company will reply within 20 working days. If the customer is not satisfied, they may contact the independent consumer ombudsman, whose decision binds the company but not the customer.

**8. Vulnerable customers.** A customer who tells the company that they, or someone in their home, depend on a reliable water supply for medical reasons, or who are over 75, may join the priority register. Registered customers receive a text message before planned interruptions and a bottled water delivery if the supply is off for more than six hours. The company reviews the register every two years and removes any entry if the customer does not reply to a letter within 28 days. The company is not liable for losses caused by an interruption that it has warned about at least 24 hours in advance, nor for any interruption caused by a burst main that is outside its reasonable control.

**9. Switching and moving house.** A customer who moves house must give the company at least five working days' notice and a final meter reading. Instalment plans do not transfer to the new address. Any remaining balance is due on the date of the final bill, and the plan fee is not refunded.

**10. Data.** The company stores meter readings, payment history and contact details for seven years after the account is closed and may share them with credit reference agencies and collection agencies.
