**1. Purpose**
Agree how we get "Foghorn", our new 4.8% amber lager, into cans and into the first 40 pubs and shops by Saturday 24 October, which is the date of the town beer festival. Marta said the recipe is locked and the pilot batch tasted right.

**2. Brewing and capacity**
Marta wants two full brews of 2,400 litres each, giving about 4,400 litres after losses and roughly 13,000 cans of 33 cl. The 2,400 litre fermenter number 3 is free from 14 September, but it is booked for the winter stout from 12 October, so lager must be out of it by then. Lager needs about 21 days from pitching to packaging-ready. Dev pointed out that 14 September plus 21 days is 5 October, which leaves one week of slack but only if both brews go in back to back. The second brew would need fermenter 2, which Marta says is free from 16 September. Nobody was sure the cold room can hold two batches at once. Dev will measure it.

**3. Packaging**
Tomasz has a quote from a mobile canning company: 1,900 pounds per day, one day covers up to 15,000 cans. The company is only available on 8 October or 15 October. Can ends and bodies have a four week lead time, so the order has to be placed by 10 September to be safe for 8 October. Tomasz proposed ordering 16,000 cans to cover waste. Priya said the budget for packaging is 6,500 pounds in total including labels. Tomasz thinks the cans alone at 0.21 pounds each come to 3,360 pounds, plus the canning day, plus labels at about 900 pounds, which is 6,160 pounds. Priya will check that against the budget line and confirm.

The label artwork was drawn by an outside illustrator and is approved except for the alcohol-unit panel, which must show 1.6 units per can. Ilse says the printer needs final files by 18 September. Joss asked that the taproom get a separate tap badge and 200 beer mats. Cost for these was not discussed.

**5. Sales**

Gareth normally handles deliveries to outlying pubs, but he is away until 21 September, so Ilse will ask whether Dev can cover route planning. Dev said he can plan routes but cannot drive, because the van is needed for kegs on Thursdays and Fridays.

**6. Festival**
Joss will run the stall. The festival wants a stall fee of 280 pounds paid by 30 September and a list of staff by 15 October. We need three people on the stall for both days. So far only Joss and Ilse have said yes.
