## Our policy notes (internal)

- Adaptation: new varifocal wearers get a free adjustment check and, if the problem continues after two weeks, a free re-test and a one-time free remake of the lenses within 60 days of collection. A remake needs an optometrist to confirm the prescription first, so we cannot change the strength without a re-test. A mobile optician service covers addresses within 12 miles of any branch for a visit fee of 25, waived for customers over 75 or with mobility problems.
- Contact lenses: wrong item shipped is always replaced free of charge with next day delivery, and the wrong box is collected by courier. Eye discomfort must be passed to the clinical team, who call within four hours. Advise the customer to stop wearing lenses and to seek urgent care if there is pain, redness or reduced vision. Refunds are not offered if a customer wore the wrong lenses; instead we replace and, if the customer wishes, offer a free check.
- Frames: the two year guarantee covers breakage from faulty materials and workmanship, not accidental damage. Hinge or joint failure under normal cleaning is normally treated as a defect. Customers can post frames using a prepaid envelope we send; repair takes about 10 working days.
- Missed appointments caused by us: we apologise, offer a free upgrade of the anti-glare coating or a 15 voucher, and the branch manager must call back within two working days.
