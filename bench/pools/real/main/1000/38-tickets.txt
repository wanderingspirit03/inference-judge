OUR RULES (internal, summarised)
- Minimum rest before a duty period is 10 hours measured from hotel arrival to pick-up. Reduction to 9 hours is allowed only for operational reasons, must be approved in advance by the Head of Flight Operations and is compensated with an extra rest day within 7 days. Any crew member may declare themselves unfit due to fatigue; a fatigue report must be filed within 24 hours, and no penalty applies.
- Swaps are approved by the scheduling team if both crew are qualified and the request is at least 7 days before. Exceptions are possible for family emergencies and weddings with a document, approved by the scheduling lead, Priyanka Wells. Leave requests must be answered within 14 days.
- Roster errors: crew members must be contacted within 4 working hours. Qualification records are held by the training department, not scheduling; scheduling can mark a currency as pending for up to 5 days.
- A trainee on a flight is a supernumerary and does not count towards minimum cabin crew. The minimum for a flight of this type is 3. Cancellation decisions belong to the Operations Control Centre, not to scheduling.
