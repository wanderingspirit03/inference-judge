I run a small business and our tax advisor sent us this engagement policy to sign. Please explain it in plain language, clause by clause, tell me what each one means for me in practice, and point out the risks or unfair terms I should question before signing. At the end, give me a short list of changes to ask for.

HOLLINGBROOK & ASH TAX ADVISORY: CLIENT ENGAGEMENT TERMS

1. Scope. We will prepare your annual accounts, your corporation tax return and, if requested, your quarterly sales-tax filings. Anything else, including advice on restructuring, investigations by the tax authority, or payroll, is outside the scope and is charged separately.

2. Your responsibilities. You must give us complete and accurate records by the dates we specify. We are entitled to rely on the information you give us and will not verify it unless we agree otherwise in writing. You are responsible for the accuracy of the returns we file on your behalf, and you must review them before we submit them.

3. Deadlines. If you send records after the date we set, we do not guarantee that we will meet the statutory filing deadline. Any late-filing penalties arising from late records are your responsibility.

4. Fees. Our fixed fee for the year is stated in the engagement letter and is payable in twelve equal monthly instalments by direct debit. Fees are reviewed each January and may increase by up to 8 percent without further notice. Work beyond the scope is charged at 135 per hour, with a minimum of one hour.

5. Late payment. Invoices unpaid after 14 days incur interest at 1.5 percent per month. We may stop work, and we may keep your records and files until all outstanding sums have been paid.

6. Termination. You may end this agreement with 90 days' written notice. We may end it with 30 days' notice, or immediately if you give us false information or fail to pay. If you end it before the end of the financial year, the remaining monthly instalments for that year remain payable.

7. Liability. Our total liability to you for any claim, however it arises, is limited to the fees you paid in the preceding twelve months. We are not liable for indirect loss or loss of profits. We are not liable for penalties or interest arising from information you gave us that turns out to be wrong or incomplete.

8. Tax advice. Unless we give you advice in writing, no statement we make in conversation or email may be relied upon as tax advice. We are not responsible for changes in tax law after the date of our advice.

9. Confidentiality and data. We keep your information confidential. We may share it with subcontractors who help prepare your work, including firms located outside the country, under written confidentiality terms. We keep records for 7 years after the end of the engagement and then destroy them.

10. Complaints. Contact the partner responsible within 30 days. If we cannot resolve it, you may refer the matter to the arbitration service named in the engagement letter, and its decision is final.

11. Changes. We may change these terms by posting the new version on our website; continued use of our services counts as acceptance.

12. Tax authority enquiries. If the tax authority opens an enquiry into a return we filed, we will tell you promptly, but responding to it is outside our scope. We will provide our working papers on request. Work on an enquiry is charged at our hourly rate, and you remain liable for any additional tax, interest and penalties, whatever their cause. We recommend that you buy tax-enquiry insurance and do not provide it ourselves.

13. Law. These terms are governed by the laws of the country where our office is located.
