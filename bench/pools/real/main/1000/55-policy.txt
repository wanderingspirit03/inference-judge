Our cement plant has just put this contractor safety policy in front of the small companies who work on site, and several of them asked me what it really means. Please explain it clause by clause in plain language, and then list the risks and obligations a small contractor should watch out for.

**Stonebridge Cement Works: Contractor Safety and Site Access Policy (version 3.1)**

**1. Scope.** This policy applies to every contractor, subcontractor and visitor who enters the Stonebridge Cement Works ("the Site") to perform work, including their employees, agents and any person acting under their direction.

**2. Induction.** No person may enter any operational area of the Site until that person has completed the Company's safety induction, which lasts approximately three hours and is valid for twelve months. The Contractor shall bear the cost of its employees' time. A person who has not attended the induction in the last twelve months may be refused entry without notice and without liability to the Company.

**3. Permits to work.** Work in confined spaces, at height above two metres, on energised equipment, or involving hot work requires a written permit issued by the Site Safety Officer before work begins. Permits are valid for one shift only. The Contractor must return each permit signed as closed at the end of the shift, and failure to do so is a breach of this policy.

**4. Personal protective equipment.** The Contractor shall supply, at its own expense, hard hats, safety boots, high-visibility clothing, eye protection and hearing protection to all its personnel, and respiratory protection where cement dust exceeds the exposure limits notified by the Company. The Company may inspect equipment at any time and may order the removal of any person whose equipment it considers inadequate.

**5. Substances and conduct.** Alcohol and non-prescribed drugs are prohibited. The Company may require any person on Site to take a breath or saliva test after an incident or on reasonable suspicion. A positive or refused test results in immediate removal and may result in termination of the Contractor's agreement.

**6. Incidents.** The Contractor must report any injury, near miss or damage to the Site Safety Officer within one hour. A written report must follow within 24 hours. The Contractor shall cooperate fully with any investigation and shall not alter the scene except to prevent further harm.

**7. Liability and insurance.** The Contractor is responsible for all loss, injury or damage caused by its personnel on the Site, except to the extent caused by the Company's gross negligence. The Contractor shall hold public liability insurance of at least 5,000,000 euros per event and employer's liability insurance as required by law, and shall provide certificates before the first day on Site and on each renewal.

**8. Suspension and termination.** The Company may suspend work immediately where it considers there is a serious safety risk. Time lost through suspension is not compensated unless the suspension was caused solely by the Company. The Company may terminate the agreement with seven days' notice for a repeated breach of this policy, or with immediate effect for a serious breach.

**9. Subcontracting.** The Contractor shall not engage a subcontractor to work on the Site without the Company's prior written consent. The Contractor remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of every subcontractor as if they were its own, and must ensure that each subcontractor has received this policy and completed the induction before arriving.

**10. Photographs and information.** Photography and the recording of video or audio on the Site are prohibited without a permit from the Site Safety Officer. The Contractor shall treat all information about the Company's processes, drawings and production data as confidential, and this duty continues for five years after the end of the agreement.

**11. Changes.** The Company may amend this policy by publishing a new version on its contractor portal. Continued presence on the Site after publication counts as acceptance.
