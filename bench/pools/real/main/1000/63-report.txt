Here is the committee's end-of-season report for the rowing club. Could you summarise it section by section for the members' newsletter and then give your recommendations on what the committee should do first? Keep in mind that most members only read the newsletter on their phone.

**Thamesbend Rowing Club: Season Report, April to September**
Prepared by: Honor Fitzgerald, Club Secretary, for the Annual General Meeting

**1. Membership**

At 30 September the club had 214 members: 118 seniors, 41 juniors, 33 social members and 22 masters. This is up from 196 a year ago. The "Learn to Row" course ran four times and took in 62 adults, of whom 29 joined as full members. Retention remains the weak spot. Of the 71 people who joined in the previous season, 38 renewed, a rate of 54%. The membership officer reports that most leavers cite early morning sessions and lack of crew places.

**2. Racing**

The club entered 11 regattas and won 19 medals, against 14 last year. Highlights were the junior girls' quad winning the regional championship in June and the men's masters eight finishing third at the national masters' regatta. The women's senior squad had a difficult season, with only six active rowers after two injuries and a house move, and was unable to enter a full crew for the autumn head race. The coaching group would like to recruit a second paid coach for the juniors, because the single junior coach now supervises up to 28 young rowers per session.

**3. Boats and equipment**

The fleet comprises 31 boats. Two single sculls and the coxed four were damaged in the July storm, when a tree fell on the boathouse annexe. The insurer paid 9,400 euros against repairs of 11,250. The eight "Lady Marian" is now 17 years old and needs a new set of riggers and a hull inspection, estimated at 3,800 euros. The club's launch has had two engine failures, one of which required a rescue by another club. The safety officer recommends replacing the engine before next spring.

**4. Facilities**

The boathouse roof leaks above the ergometer room. A survey in August quoted 24,000 euros for a full replacement or 6,500 euros for a temporary repair that would last about three winters. The landlord, the town council, has said it will contribute up to a third of a full replacement if the club applies by the end of the year. The clubhouse bar made a profit of 4,100 euros, mainly from the summer regatta weekend.

**5. Finances**

Income was 142,300 euros (subscriptions 96,800, grants 18,200, bar and events 21,500, other 5,800) and expenditure was 138,950, leaving a surplus of 3,350 euros. Reserves stand at 41,000, which covers about four months of costs. The treasurer proposes a subscription rise of 4% for seniors from January and no change for juniors.

**6. Safety and welfare**

There were no serious incidents. Three capsizes were recorded, all in cold water in March, all with a launch present. The welfare officer completed the annual training for all coaches, but two volunteer coxes still lack the first aid certificate.
