# VOLUNTEER SERVICES AND VENUE LICENCE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 2 September between **Saltmarsh Film Festival CIC** ("the Festival"), whose office is at 14 Quay Lane, and **Harbourlight Cinemas Ltd** ("the Venue Owner"), whose registered office is at 3 Grain Street.

## 1. Definitions

"Festival Period" means the eleven days from 9 October to 19 October inclusive. "Premises" means Screens 1, 2 and 4, the foyer and the upstairs bar area of the Venue Owner's cinema at 3 Grain Street. "Volunteers" means individuals recruited by the Festival who give their time without payment. "Venue Staff" means employees of the Venue Owner.

## 2. Licence

2.1 The Venue Owner grants the Festival a non-exclusive licence to use the Premises during the Festival Period between 09:00 and 23:30 each day for the public screening of films and related talks.

2.2 The Festival may not sub-license the Premises or allow a third party to run any event there without written consent, which the Venue Owner will not unreasonably withhold.

2.3 Screen 3 and the café remain under the Venue Owner's control. The Festival may not place signs or equipment in either.

## 3. Fees

3.1 The Festival shall pay a licence fee of 18,000, in two instalments: 9,000 on signing and 9,000 no later than 5 October.

3.2 If the Festival fails to pay an instalment within seven days of the due date, the Venue Owner may suspend access to the Premises until the sum is paid, and the Festival remains liable for the full fee.

3.3 The Venue Owner keeps all income from food and drink. The Festival keeps all ticket income, less a booking charge of 4 percent which the Venue Owner collects through its ticketing system and pays over within 30 days after the Festival Period.

## 4. Volunteers

4.1 The Festival shall provide up to 60 Volunteers. Each shift lasts no more than six hours without a break of at least 30 minutes.

4.2 The Festival is responsible for recruiting Volunteers, checking that they are at least 16 years old, and giving them a safety briefing before their first shift. Volunteers under 18 shall not work in the upstairs bar area or after 22:00.

4.3 Volunteers are not employees or workers of the Venue Owner, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship. The Venue Owner may give reasonable instructions to Volunteers about safety, but shall not direct them to do work normally done by Venue Staff, such as handling cash in the café or operating projectors.

4.4 The Festival shall keep a record of each Volunteer's name, contact details, shift times and briefing date, and shall give the record to the Venue Owner on request.

## 5. Insurance and liability

5.1 The Festival shall hold public liability insurance of at least 2,000,000 for the Festival Period and shall give the Venue Owner a copy of the certificate by 25 September.

5.2 The Festival shall indemnify the Venue Owner against all claims, losses and costs arising from the acts or omissions of the Festival or its Volunteers at the Premises, except to the extent that they are caused by the negligence of the Venue Owner or Venue Staff.

5.3 The Venue Owner's total liability to the Festival under this Agreement, whether in contract or otherwise, shall not exceed the licence fee paid. Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

5.4 The Venue Owner is not liable for loss of, or damage to, equipment or property belonging to the Festival or Volunteers kept at the Premises.

## 6. Cancellation

6.1 If the Festival cancels all or part of the Festival Period more than 60 days before it begins, it shall pay 25 percent of the licence fee. If it cancels within 60 days, it shall pay the full fee.

6.2 If the Venue Owner cannot make the Premises available for reasons within its control, it shall refund the fee for each day lost and may be required to pay the Festival's reasonable costs of finding a substitute venue, up to 5,000.

6.3 Neither party is liable for failure to perform caused by events outside its reasonable control, including fire, flood, government order or the closure of the Premises on police advice. In that case the Venue Owner shall refund the fee for the days lost, but the Festival shall not be entitled to any other compensation.

## 7. Data and publicity

7.1 Each party shall comply with data protection law in relation to personal data it holds about Volunteers and audiences. The Venue Owner shall not use Volunteer contact details for marketing.

7.2 The Venue Owner may use the Festival's name and photographs taken in public areas for its own promotion until the end of the following year.

## 7A. Safety and access

7A.1 The Venue Owner shall ensure that fire exits, alarms and emergency lighting in the Premises are in working order throughout the Festival Period, and shall give the Festival's safety lead a copy of the current fire risk assessment by 25 September.

7A.2 The Festival shall appoint a named safety lead for each day of the Festival Period. The safety lead shall brief all Volunteers on evacuation routes and shall be present in the Premises whenever the public is admitted.

7A.3 The Venue Owner confirms that Screens 1 and 2 have step-free access and a wheelchair space in each row marked "A". Screen 4 is reached by a staircase only. The Festival shall not schedule events in Screen 4 which it has advertised as step-free.

7A.4 The maximum number of people in each room is the number stated on the Venue Owner's licence from the local council. The Festival shall not sell more tickets than that number for any screening.

## 8. Termination and general

8.1 Either party may end this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other commits a serious breach that cannot be put right, or does not put right another breach within 14 days of written notice.

8.2 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales. Any dispute shall first be discussed by the directors of both parties for 21 days before either may start court proceedings.

8.3 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by post or by email to the address at the head of this Agreement. A notice sent by email is treated as received on the next working day. Changes to this Agreement are only valid if both parties sign them.
