Notes from the workshop:
1. Job H-2305 was the large fitted kitchen for a housing association show flat. The customer changed the worktop material in week three, which cost us about 20 hours of rework that was not charged.
2. Job H-2323 ran six days over because the oak for the doors arrived late from the merchant.
3. We had two people off sick for part of the month, and one apprentice, Tobias, started on the 9th.
4. We raised our standard prices on tables and chairs by 6 percent on 1 September. Cabinets and beds were not changed.
5. Restoration jobs are usually quoted at a fixed price after a short visit. The estimator, Wilf, did all of them in September.
6. The web enquiry form was rebuilt in August and now asks the customer for a rough budget. Before that it did not.
7. Two jobs from August that finished in September are in the table. These are H-2301 and H-2302, and they were priced at the old rates.
8. September had 22 working days, and we paid for about 1,950 staff hours in total across the twelve people, including the apprentice from the 9th. Holiday and sickness are included in that paid time, and so are the hours spent on quoting, ordering and sweeping up, which are not booked to any job.
